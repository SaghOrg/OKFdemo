# Project Bharadwaj Glossary

Same fact, six vocabularies. This file exists because Vikram Sethi's numbers "come different" partly
for a real data reason (VAR-004, VAR-007...) and partly because he is quoting a different *thing*
than everyone else is quoting, in a different *word* than everyone else uses. Translate before you
trust a discrepancy.

**How to use this file.** Each section below is one canonical concept. Every section carries a single
`Aliases:` line — grep that line and you will hit every synonym, register variant, and ASR
mis-transcription of the term in one match. Speaker attributions were confirmed by grepping the
transcripts and emails directly (`_sources/`, mirrored as plain text under `_build/corpus_text/`), not
copied blind from `_canon/terminology_map.csv`. Where the corpus disagreed with the canon's binding
claim, or with itself, that is called out inline rather than smoothed over.

**A transcription-fidelity note that affects every section below:** the `.vtt` files are the raw,
speaker-diarized ASR output (`<v Speaker Name>text`). The `.txt` files in the same `meetings/` folder
are humans' cleaned-up circulated notes of the *same* meeting, and they do not always agree with the
`.vtt` on who said what — see the callout under **Dashboard** for a concrete case where trusting the
`.txt` gives you the wrong speaker entirely. When attribution matters, check the `.vtt`.

## Distributor

BCPL's word for the wholesale customer who buys product from BCPL and resells it to retail. The
canonical term used by the large majority of the corpus.

**Aliases:** distributor, channel partner, stockist, party, party master, party code, cust code, DIST-code (e.g. `DIST-W-0241`), the trade *(see note)*

- **Distributor** — canonical. Used by nearly everyone: Ananya Krishnan, Karthik Subramanian, Ishaan
  Bhatt, Neha Gokhale, Ritwik Ghosh, Sneha Pillai, Shalini Iyer, Priya Nair, Meghna Rao, Rajeev Menon,
  Farida Contractor, Aniruddh Deshpande.
- **Channel partner** — Klarissen Group's word, always. Marijke van der Berg and Wei Lin Tan never say
  "distributor" and never say "stockist". Confirmed repeatedly, e.g. Marijke: *"The India channel
  partner credit notes are not in the warehouse at all."* (`/_sources/email/EM-061_marijke_credit_note_escalation.eml`)
  and in the kickoff, *"the channel partner side the deductions the promo accrual"*
  (`/_sources/meetings/2026-02-11_kickoff_scope.txt`).
- **Stockist** — Vikram Sethi's word, always, without exception, in every artifact. He never says
  "distributor" and never says "channel partner". Confirmed in `/_sources/chat/CH-02_whatsapp_uat_group.txt`
  (UAT log) and `/_sources/meetings/2026-08-05_dashboard_scope_workshop.vtt` ("the stockist level
  numbers", "three forty odd stock lists" — see the ASR entry below).
- **Party / party master / party code** — Aniruddh "Ani" Deshpande's trade usage, inherited from the
  ORION schema (`CUSTOMER` is informally "the party master"; `CUST_CODE` is "the party code"). Confirmed
  in `/_sources/chat/CH-02_whatsapp_uat_group.txt` ("that party code vikram is talking about...") and
  `/_sources/docs/DOC-01_orion_oltp_schema_notes.docx` ("4. CUSTOMER (the party master)"). Ishaan Bhatt
  picks up "party code" too when discussing `DIST-W-0241` — confirmed in
  `/_sources/email/EM-055_var004_handover_ishaan.eml`. `DK-02` explicitly flags this as Ani's
  long-standing habit, not a separate table (`/_sources/decks/DK-02_discovery_findings.pptx`).
- **"The trade"** — listed in `_canon/terminology_map.csv` as a Vikram Sethi/Rajeev Menon register
  variant for distributor, but this was **not confirmed** as a standalone usage anywhere in the corpus
  on this pass. The only "trade" hits found are "trade promotion accrual" (Shalini's term, a different
  concept — see **Scheme discount** below), "trade parties" (a one-off in DOC-01), and Ani's own
  "modern trade" as a *channel type* distinct from distributor
  (`/_sources/meetings/2026-03-03_oltp_discovery_ani.vtt`: "three values, distributor, modern trade,
  institutional"). Treat "the trade" = distributor as unconfirmed if you go looking for it.

## Primary sales

**BCPL selling to the distributor.** This is an invoice. It is revenue. Grain: BCPL → distributor.

**Aliases:** primary sales, primary, sell-in, billing, invoiced sales, despatch sales

- **Primary sales** — canonical, used by Karthik Subramanian, Ananya Krishnan, Shalini Iyer, Ishaan
  Bhatt, Neha Gokhale, Sneha Pillai, Ritwik Ghosh.
- **Primary** (bare noun) — Vikram Sethi and Priya Nair's abbreviation.
- **Sell-in** — Klarissen's word (Wei Lin Tan, Marijke van der Berg). Confirmed:
  *"sell in is what you are calling primary... when india sends a number and it is labelled sales we
  assume sell in"* — Wei Lin Tan, `/_sources/meetings/2026-08-05_dashboard_scope_workshop.txt`.
- **Billing** — Vikram Sethi's word, and it is a misuse in the sense that he uses it interchangeably
  with primary sales without always being precise about invoiced-vs-cancelled lines. His own
  definition, confirmed in `.vtt`: *"billing means what we have invoiced to the stock list"* — Vikram
  Sethi, `/_sources/meetings/2026-08-05_dashboard_scope_workshop.vtt`.
- **Invoiced sales** — Shalini Iyer's finance-register phrase, and the phrase she uses to draw the line
  against Vikram's off-take number: *"the pack i sign is on invoiced sales, not on off take"* — Shalini
  Iyer, `/_sources/meetings/2026-03-24_first_variance_findings.txt`.
- **Despatch sales** — legacy term used by Farida Contractor and Aniruddh Deshpande (ORION's
  `DOC_TYPE='INV'` origin). Not confirmed as a standalone phrase on this grep pass outside the canon
  list; treat as a legacy synonym rather than an actively-quoted one.

## Secondary sales

**The distributor reselling to the retailer.** This is NOT a BCPL invoice and is NOT revenue. It comes
from a separate, partial feed (see **Secondary sales feed / DMS feed** below), not from ORION.

**Aliases:** secondary sales, secondary, sell-out, off-take, retail off-take

- **Secondary sales** — canonical, used by Karthik Subramanian, Ananya Krishnan, Ishaan Bhatt, Neha
  Gokhale, Sneha Pillai, Ritwik Ghosh, Meghna Rao.
- **Secondary** (bare noun) — Vikram Sethi and Priya Nair's abbreviation.
- **Sell-out** — Klarissen's word. Confirmed, Wei Lin Tan: *"sell in and sell out... sell out is what
  you are calling secondary"* (`/_sources/meetings/2026-08-05_dashboard_scope_workshop.txt`); also
  Marijke to Vikram (misnamed "Vikrant" by ASR — see **People**), Wei Lin Tan:
  *"can you send me your sell out view for the north"* (`/_sources/meetings/2026-09-22_steering_committee.txt`).
- **Off-take / retail off-take** — Vikram Sethi's word, always. Confirmed repeatedly: *"my figuers for
  west stockist off-take are not matching"* (`/_sources/chat/CH-02_whatsapp_uat_group.txt`, note his
  characteristic uncorrected typo "figuers").

### PRIMARY vs SECONDARY — they are not the same thing, read this before trusting a "mismatch"

This is the single most common source of "my number is coming different" in this engagement, and it is
worth stating flatly:

> **Primary sales = BCPL billing the distributor = revenue.**
> **Secondary sales = the distributor selling on to the retailer = not revenue, not in the warehouse's
> revenue fact, and only ~74% covered by the feed that exists at all.**

Vikram Sethi routinely quotes secondary sales (his "off-take") while everyone else in the room is
quoting primary sales (invoiced/billing), and the two numbers legitimately never tie — that is not a
defect to fix, it is two different measures being compared as if they were one. Karthik Subramanian
makes the correction explicitly and by name:

> *"Vikram, off-take is not the same thing as billing. If your sheet is off-take and the page is
> showing invoiced sales they will never agree, and that is correct behaviour rather than a defect."*
> — Karthik Subramanian, `/_sources/chat/CH-02_whatsapp_uat_group.txt`

Karthik Subramanian makes the same point in writing, unprompted, in the target model notes:

> *"primary sales and secondary sales are not the same measure. Primary is BCPL billing the
> distributor. Secondary is the distributor selling on to the retailer. Only the first of those is
> revenue."*
> — `/_sources/docs/DOC-05_edw_target_model_notes.docx`

Sneha Pillai's meeting notes record the same conflation happening again later, without a name attached
to who was corrected: *"he is comparing secondary against primary again i have told him i have told
him more than once"* (`/_sources/meetings/2026-06-18_dq_readout.txt`) — read in context this is about
Vikram, consistent with the pattern above. Ritwik Ghosh's dashboard wireframe pack flags the same risk
at the design level, warning against ever plotting the two on one visual: *"blending a partial
secondary feed onto revenue pages puts two truths on one page"* (`/_sources/decks/DK-05_dashboard_wireframes.pptx`).

**Do not** resolve a variance by assuming both parties mean the same "sales" number. Ask which one
first.

## Scheme discount

The trade promotion / discount BCPL gives a distributor, computed nightly against cumulative slab
entitlement (`FIN_PROD.PKG_MONTH_END` → `P_RECALC_SCHEME_DISCOUNT`). Five live scheme types in the
source (`SCHEME_MASTER`): QPS, MONTHLY, SLAB, TPR.

**Aliases:** scheme discount, trade promotion accrual, promo accrual, scheme accrual, TPR, secondary scheme, the scheme amount, discount

- **Scheme discount** — canonical, used by Karthik Subramanian, Ishaan Bhatt, Aniruddh Deshpande,
  Ananya Krishnan, Priya Nair, Meghna Rao, Ritwik Ghosh, Sneha Pillai.
- **Trade promotion accrual / scheme accrual** — Shalini Iyer's finance term, always, quoted to two
  decimals with a period attached. Confirmed heavily across meetings, e.g.
  `/_sources/meetings/2026-08-05_dashboard_scope_workshop.txt` ("for us net revenue is invoiced sales
  less trade promotion accrual...") and `/_sources/meetings/2026-03-24_first_variance_findings.txt`.
  A speaker-notes aside in `DK-02` records that Shalini specifically does **not** use the word "delta"
  and prefers "reconciliation gap" — see **Variance** below
  (`/_sources/decks/DK-02_discovery_findings.pptx`).
- **Promo accrual** — Klarissen's word (Marijke van der Berg, Wei Lin Tan).
- **TPR / secondary scheme** — Vikram Sethi's loose, catch-all usage for anything promotional (canon
  marks this "misuse" — he does not distinguish TPR from other scheme types). Confirmed:
  *"also the TPR amount is not matching for nimbua south"* pattern recurs across
  `/_sources/chat/CH-02_whatsapp_uat_group.txt` and `/_sources/email/EM-084_north_file_too_big.eml`.
  Farida Contractor also says "the scheme amount" / "secondary scheme" — confirmed in her vocabulary
  per `_canon/terminology_map.csv`; her artifacts consistently avoid "variance" in favour of
  "reconciliation" (see below).
- **Discount** (bare) — Rajeev Menon's register-level shorthand when he is not being precise.

## Variance

A discrepancy between two reported numbers for what should be the same fact — most often, the
warehouse's revenue figure against Finance's trial balance.

**Aliases:** variance, reconciliation gap, delta, mismatch, not matching, the walk, bridge, reconciliation, reconciliation issue

- **Variance** — canonical, used by Ananya Krishnan, Karthik Subramanian, Rajeev Menon, Sneha Pillai,
  Neha Gokhale, Ishaan Bhatt.
- **Reconciliation gap** — Shalini Iyer's word, always, and deliberately **not** "delta". A speaker-note
  in the discovery deck spells this out explicitly for anyone drafting on her behalf: *"Use
  'reconciliation gap' if she is in the room, she does not use the word delta."*
  (`/_sources/decks/DK-02_discovery_findings.pptx`). Confirmed in her own words: *"i have a
  reconciliation gap sitting open in a pack that group has already seen"*
  (`/_sources/meetings/2026-03-24_first_variance_findings.txt`).
- **Delta** — Aniruddh Deshpande and Ishaan Bhatt's jargon. Also the literal spreadsheet label in
  `/_sources/trackers/XL-03_scd2_load_impact.xlsx` ("Measured delta").
- **Mismatch / not matching** — Vikram Sethi's register ("my number is coming different" is the same
  idea in his own phrasing); Priya Nair also says "not matching".
- **The walk** — Marijke van der Berg's phrase for a variance explained end-to-end, gross to net.
  Confirmed: *"whatever you build i want the walk from gross to net to be visible"*
  (`/_sources/meetings/2026-02-11_kickoff_scope.txt`); also *"And the walk from what the warehouse
  reports to what your Finance team actually books."* (`/_sources/email/EM-061_marijke_credit_note_escalation.eml`).
- **Bridge** — Wei Lin Tan's word for the same idea, usually requested as a deliverable: *"Can you send
  the bridge?"* (`/_sources/email/EM-061_marijke_credit_note_escalation.eml`, quoting Wei Lin's
  original ask; also `/_sources/meetings/2026-08-05_dashboard_scope_workshop.txt`).
- **Reconciliation / reconciliation issue** — Farida Contractor's preferred word over "variance" when
  she can use it, and Shalini Iyer uses it too in the general sense. Confirmed: *"That is a
  reconciliation issue and I am raising it as one."* — Farida Contractor,
  `/_sources/chat/CH-01_teams_data_workstream.txt`.
- **Internal register warning:** a Klarissen person (Marijke, Wei Lin) on the call is the trigger for
  using "the walk" / "the bridge" language; BCPL-internal working sessions use "variance" instead. A
  speaker-note makes this explicit: *"If someone from Klarissen is on this call... do not use 'the
  walk' or 'the bridge', that is their phrasing, use variance."* (`/_sources/decks/DK-03_variance_rootcause_v2.pptx`)

**Note — Meghna Rao / Neha Gokhale's DQ-specific sense of "variance":** in Data Quality usage,
"variance" sometimes means a rule-failure count rather than a financial discrepancy, which is a
separate, narrower usage from the finance sense above and is a known source of cross-team confusion
(per `_canon/terminology_map.csv`; not independently re-confirmed with a corpus quote on this pass —
flagged here so a future agent knows to check before conflating "Meghna's variance" with "Shalini's
variance").

## Credit note

A downward revenue adjustment issued to a distributor (rate differences, off-invoice adjustments,
returns). **There is no credit note fact table in the warehouse** — Finance nets these in Excel outside
the EDW (VAR-005).

**Aliases:** credit note, CN, claim, rate difference

- **Credit note** — canonical, used by Shalini Iyer, Karthik Subramanian, Ananya Krishnan, Marijke van
  der Berg, Ishaan Bhatt, Sneha Pillai.
- **CN** — abbreviation, Shalini Iyer and Farida Contractor.
- **Rate difference** — Shalini Iyer's term for one specific credit-note type (the validated FY26 figure
  of INR 3.11 Cr is explicitly scoped to "rate difference and off-invoice adjustment types only" —
  `/_sources/decks/DK-06_steerco_sep2026.pptx`).
- **Claim** — Vikram Sethi's misuse, meaning what the distributor sees rather than what Finance books.
  Confirmed in the raw transcript: *`<v Vikram Sethi>they see the claim, ma'am`*
  (`/_sources/meetings/2026-03-24_first_variance_findings.vtt`). **Attribution caveat:** the circulated
  `.txt` notes for this same meeting mislabel this exact line as spoken by **Shalini Iyer**
  ("the distributors do not see either of these numbers vikram they see the claim ma'am" — one
  merged line, wrong speaker tag,
  `/_sources/meetings/2026-03-24_first_variance_findings.txt`). The `.vtt` is correct and matches the
  canon's binding claim that "claim" is Vikram's word; the `.txt` is the one in error here. Use the
  `.vtt` for attribution.

## Secondary sales feed

The upstream data source for secondary sales, distinct from ORION. Structurally incomplete: covers
roughly 74% of distributors and lags about five days behind. Feeds `FACT_SECONDARY_SALES`.

**Aliases:** DMS feed, SFA upload, secondary file

- **DMS feed** — canonical among the consultants (Ishaan Bhatt, Karthik Subramanian). Confirmed:
  *"the d m s feed that gives us secondary sales, it does not cover all the distributors, it is coming
  in at about seventy four percent"* — Ishaan Bhatt, `/_sources/meetings/2026-03-24_first_variance_findings.vtt`;
  the same 74% figure and "structurally incomplete" characterization appears in
  `/_sources/docs/DOC-05_edw_target_model_notes.docx`.
- **SFA upload** — Farida Contractor and Priya Nair's legacy name for the same feed.
- **The secondary file** — Vikram Sethi's register for it.

## Nightly load

The ETL run that populates the warehouse each night. Triggered by Control-M job
`BCPL_EDW_DAILY_LOAD_START` at **01:00 IST every day including Sundays**, load plan `LP_DAILY_SALES`,
typically done with post-load checks by **~02:05 IST**. See `_canon/BRIEF.md` §8.1 for the frozen
timing numbers — this glossary only covers the words for it.

**Aliases:** nightly load, the run, the batch, load plan, the job

- **Nightly load** — canonical, Karthik Subramanian, Ananya Krishnan, Neha Gokhale, Sneha Pillai,
  Ishaan Bhatt.
- **The run** — Farida Contractor and Aniruddh Deshpande's jargon. Confirmed constantly, e.g. Farida:
  *"the run is typically done around 02:05"* (`/_sources/chat/CH-01_teams_data_workstream.txt`); Ani:
  *"will check tomorrow morning after the run"* (same file, his sign-off habit).
- **The batch** — Rajeev Menon, Shalini Iyer, Vikram Sethi's register.
- **Load plan** — Farida Contractor and Karthik Subramanian's precise term for `LP_DAILY_SALES` itself.
- **The job** — Priya Nair and Vikram Sethi's register.

**Related term — idempotent:** Karthik Subramanian's word for "safe to re-run without duplicating
data" (the property `LP_DAILY_SALES` lacks — this is the VAR-008/VAR-003 mechanism). Confirmed: *"The
load plan is not idempotent, which is a design problem, not an operator problem."*
(`/_sources/chat/CH-01_teams_data_workstream.txt`). ASR renders it "I dem potent" — see **ASR
mis-transcriptions** below.
