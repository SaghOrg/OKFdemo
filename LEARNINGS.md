Reviving the credit-note extract — the fix `VAR-005` has been open on since the start — reopens the exact load-window pressure that got `MAP_STG_CREDIT_NOTE` disabled on 14-Nov-2022, and that cost has never been measured. `ADR-004`'s key-based rebuild of `MAP_FACT_INVOICE_LINE` (`/decisions/20260506-var003-remediation-key-based-merge.md`) is a second unbuilt remediation contending for the same nightly window. Neither has been sized, both are pending against the 05:30 IST availability SLA, and both land twelve days before the 12-Nov-2026 go-live, against a schedule with almost no float. **No single source document contains that connection.** It exists only because a 2022 operational decision, an unbuilt 2026 remediation, an SLA figure and a go-live date are separate concepts that link to each other — retrieval that stops at the one document answering the question asked will never surface it. The lesson for how the KB is built: cross-links are not navigation convenience, they are the mechanism by which a risk nobody thought to ask about becomes visible.
